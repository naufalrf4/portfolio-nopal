## Undergraduate thesis (August 2026)

Title: "Implementation of an IoT-Based Water Quality Monitoring System for Discus Fish Aquariums with a Risk Index". Published via IPB University, 14 August 2026, at repository.ipb.ac.id/handle/123456789/179283.

The thesis introduces a composite risk index for discus fish aquarium water quality, built on an ESP32-S3 tracking pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, and TDS. Real-time alerts fire through WhatsApp, Telegram, and push notifications via a Next.js dashboard. Testing showed the risk index rises notably before fish mortality events, which is the core contribution: it turns four independent readings into one leading indicator. Skripsi Naufal memperkenalkan indeks risiko komposit untuk kualitas air akuarium ikan discus berbasis ESP32-S3, dengan peringatan real-time lewat WhatsApp, Telegram, dan notifikasi push melalui dashboard Next.js.

## Journal article on sensor calibration (January 2025)

Title: "Calibration of Dissolved Oxygen Sensors in IoT Systems for Water Quality Monitoring in Aquaculture". Published in Spektra: Jurnal Fisika dan Aplikasinya, Volume 10 Issue 3. DOI: 10.21009/SPEKTRA.103.06.

This is co-authored research on sensor calibration accuracy for aquaculture IoT monitoring. Testing across six water media types achieved a mean absolute error of 0.1083 mg/L and an RMSE of 0.2654 mg/L, showing consistent dissolved oxygen sensor performance after calibration. Artikel jurnal ini membahas akurasi kalibrasi sensor dissolved oxygen untuk pemantauan akuakultur berbasis IoT, dengan pengujian pada enam jenis media air.

The practical significance: consumer-grade probes drift, and this quantifies how accurate they remain after calibration, which is what justifies using them in research-grade monitoring at all.

## Technical article: MQTT vs HTTP for IoT (July 2025)

Title: "MQTT vs HTTP: Choosing the Right Protocol for Internet of Things (IoT) Applications". Published on Medium under the Digistar Club publication, 28 July 2025. Written in Indonesian.

The article explains how smart devices such as temperature sensors, cameras, and automatic sprinkler systems connect, communicate, exchange data, and push information to a phone instantly, and why that depends on the choice between two widely used network protocols, MQTT and HTTP. Artikel ini menjelaskan bagaimana perangkat pintar seperti sensor suhu, kamera, dan penyiram otomatis dapat saling terhubung dan bertukar data, serta mengapa pilihan antara protokol MQTT dan HTTP menentukan hasilnya.

This is Naufal's own technical writing, and it maps directly onto his hands-on MQTT work in the SIMONAIR and ELSA IoT projects.
