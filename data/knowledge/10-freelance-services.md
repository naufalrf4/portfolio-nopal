## How Naufal runs an engagement

Naufal owns the full lifecycle himself: requirements gathering, system architecture, implementation, and end-to-end deployment. Clients do not need a written specification to start, because he does the requirements work as part of the engagement. He works remotely, in Indonesian or English, on Asia/Jakarta time (GMT+7, WIB). Naufal menangani keseluruhan siklus sendiri: penggalian kebutuhan, arsitektur sistem, implementasi, hingga deployment, dan bekerja secara remote dalam Bahasa Indonesia atau Inggris pada zona waktu WIB.

## How pricing works

Naufal does not publish a fixed rate card or fixed package prices on the portfolio. Pricing is quoted per project, based on a breakdown of the modules and features a client actually needs, so the scope drives the number rather than a generic tier. The portfolio's AI assistant can produce an indicative feature-by-feature quote breakdown when a client describes what they want built, but any figure produced that way is an estimate for discussion, not a binding offer. A firm quote comes from Naufal directly after a scoping conversation. Naufal tidak menerbitkan daftar tarif tetap; harga dihitung per proyek berdasarkan rincian modul dan fitur yang benar-benar dibutuhkan. Estimasi dari asisten AI bersifat indikatif untuk bahan diskusi, bukan penawaran final, dan harga pasti diberikan Naufal setelah pembahasan lingkup kerja.

Quotes are normally denominated in Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) for Indonesian clients. Penawaran umumnya dalam Rupiah untuk klien Indonesia.

## What affects the price of a project

The factors that move a quote are: the number and complexity of modules; whether hardware is involved, since custom hardware requires physical prototyping and procurement; whether the system must handle burst or peak traffic, as with national registration platforms; integration requirements with existing systems or third-party services; whether AI or LLM features are included; deployment and ongoing operational needs; and the deadline, since compressed timelines change what is feasible. Faktor yang memengaruhi harga: jumlah dan kompleksitas modul, keterlibatan hardware, kebutuhan menahan beban puncak, integrasi dengan sistem lain, fitur AI atau LLM, kebutuhan deployment dan operasional, serta ketatnya tenggat waktu.

For those, a specialist is the better answer. Naufal bukan pilihan yang tepat untuk pengembangan aplikasi mobile native, game development, atau data science tingkat riset.
