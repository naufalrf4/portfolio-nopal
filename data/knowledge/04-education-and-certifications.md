## Undergraduate thesis

Naufal's thesis was on IoT-based water quality monitoring for discus fish aquariums, and it capped his degree. The full title is "Implementation of an IoT-Based Water Quality Monitoring System for Discus Fish Aquariums with a Risk Index". It introduces a composite risk index for aquarium water quality, built on an ESP32-S3 tracking pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, and TDS. Real-time alerts fire through WhatsApp, Telegram, and push notifications via a Next.js dashboard, and testing showed the risk index rises notably before fish mortality events. The thesis is deposited at IPB University's repository: repository.ipb.ac.id/handle/123456789/179283. Skripsi Naufal membahas sistem pemantauan kualitas air berbasis IoT untuk akuarium ikan discus dengan indeks risiko, menggunakan ESP32-S3 dan dashboard Next.js.
