## How Naufal describes himself

Naufal is curious by default. In his own framing, he just likes figuring out how to make things easier, and that curiosity is what keeps pulling him into web development, embedded systems, and AI-augmented workflows. He learns by shipping: he tries new tools and technologies and turns whatever he learns into something that actually works rather than stopping at a tutorial. Naufal menggambarkan dirinya sebagai orang yang penasaran dan suka mencari cara membuat sesuatu jadi lebih mudah, lalu mengubah apa pun yang dipelajarinya menjadi hal yang benar-benar jalan.

## Personality type and temperament

Naufal's MBTI type is ISTJ, The Logistician. He is structured, systematic, and analytical, and impatient with inefficient processes: if there is a faster and tidier way to do something, he would rather do that. Kepribadian Naufal bertipe ISTJ, terstruktur dan sistematis, serta tidak suka proses yang tidak efisien.

## Strengths he claims for himself

Critical thinking, a structured approach to problem-solving, and adapting quickly to unfamiliar technology. He picks up new stacks fast, which is visible in his portfolio: Go and Rust both appear in shipped work despite being newer to him than his primary JavaScript and PHP stacks. Kelebihan yang ia akui: berpikir kritis, pendekatan sistematis dalam memecahkan masalah, dan cepat beradaptasi dengan teknologi baru.

## Weaknesses he admits to

Perfectionism, taking on too many things in parallel, and overthinking large decisions. He is aware of these and describes working on balance rather than claiming they are solved. Kelemahan yang ia akui: cenderung perfeksionis, mengambil terlalu banyak hal sekaligus, dan overthinking pada keputusan besar.

## How Naufal works with AI

His agentic tooling includes Claude Code, n8n for workflow automation, OpenCode, Hermes Agent, and Pi Agent Harness. This is also why his professional identity is "AI-Augmented Engineer" rather than simply full-stack developer. Naufal memakai AI sebagai bagian dari cara berpikir dan bekerjanya, bukan alat tambahan, dan inilah alasan ia menyebut dirinya AI-Augmented Engineer.

## Life philosophy

Naufal's stated philosophy is to keep learning and keep growing: every day should include something new learned, whether a skill or an insight. He believes learning has no ceiling and treats each day as a chance to be better than the previous version of himself. Filosofi hidup Naufal adalah terus belajar dan terus berkembang, karena menurutnya proses belajar tidak punya batas.
