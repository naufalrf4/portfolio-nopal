## SEMAPOR — civic reporting platform (2024)

Slug: semapor. Category: Web Development. Stack: Laravel, MySQL. SEMAPOR (Semarang Lapor) is an award-winning civic reporting platform that won 1st place at DINACOM 2024, letting citizens report public issues while city staff track resolution workflows. It streamlines civic reporting into a structured system instead of scattered complaint channels, with a two-sided workflow: public submission on one end, government resolution tracking on the other. The competition win validates both the technical execution and the practical relevance to real government use cases. SEMAPOR adalah platform pelaporan warga yang meraih Juara 1 DINACOM 2024, menghubungkan pelaporan masalah publik dengan pelacakan penyelesaian oleh petugas kota.

## Lease & Asset Management — banking enterprise system (2025)

Slug: lease-asset-management. Category: Web Development. Stack: Laravel, MySQL. Built for a bank client in Indonesia, this enterprise system manages the leasing of retail outlets and ATM units, tracking lease terms, asset locations, and placement records. It centralizes what was previously scattered across manual outlet and ATM records into a single relational database. It is the kind of unglamorous but high-stakes back-office system financial institutions depend on daily, delivered under a confidential banking engagement. Sistem enterprise untuk klien perbankan di Indonesia yang mengelola penyewaan gerai retail dan unit ATM, mencakup jangka sewa, lokasi aset, dan catatan penempatan.

## HRIS: KPI Management (2025)

Slug: hris-kpi-management. Category: Web Development. Stack: Next.js, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL. Modul HRIS untuk penetapan objektif, pelacakan KPI, dan penilaian kinerja berbasis Management by Objectives, menggantikan spreadsheet yang tersebar.

## ELSA IoT — Hardware & Sensors (2025)

Slug: elsa-iot-hardware-sensors. Category: IoT & Hardware. Live at elsaiot.web.id. An ESP32-S3-based sensor unit paired with water quality sensors, transmitting telemetry to the cloud for the ELSA web dashboard. It was designed deliberately as a lower-cost, lighter-weight alternative to SIMONAIR for users who need simpler monitoring without a full research rig. It demonstrates scaling hardware complexity up or down to match actual project requirements rather than defaulting to the same setup every time. Unit sensor berbasis ESP32-S3 sebagai alternatif SIMONAIR yang lebih ringan dan lebih murah untuk pemantauan kualitas air sederhana.

## IslamicVerse — AI-powered Islamic services site (2023)

Slug: islamicverse. Category: Web Development. Stack: static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, plus an Express.js proxy. An award-winning AI-powered static site delivering Islamic information and digital services through a secure chat proxy that forwards requests to the OpenAI API while keeping the API key server-side. It won 1st place at Festival Teknik Informatika 2023 for combining AI-enabled features with a simple, secure architecture, an early example of practical AI integration years before it became standard. The security-conscious proxy pattern, keeping secrets off the client, remains a fundamental best practice for production AI applications. IslamicVerse meraih Juara 1 Festival Teknik Informatika 2023, memadukan fitur berbasis AI dengan arsitektur sederhana yang aman lewat proxy yang menjaga API key tetap di sisi server.
